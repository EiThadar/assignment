✨ My Hobbies ✨

📖 Reading & Imagination

Books are my doorway to other worlds. I love getting lost in detective novels, psychological thrillers, and fantasy stories where each page holds a new twist. My favourite is solving the mystery before the characters do!

🎬 Drama & Mystery Lover

Whether it's Korean thrillers, Chinese suspense dramas, or Japanese crime stories, I enjoy shows that keep me thinking. I also love watching characters evolve through intense plotlines and unexpected betrayals.

📝 Writing My Own Worlds

I write stories based on game-like systems, with layers of mystery, puzzles, and emotional growth. Each story I write is like building a serect world only I know how to navigate. Writing is not just a hobby-it's my creative escape.

🎮 Games, Logic, and Strategy

I don’t just play games—I study them. I enjoy breaking down game mechanics, imagining survival rounds, and designing challenges like the ones in my favorite dramas. Strategy games and logic puzzles inspire many of my stories.

🌸 Quiet Time & Coffee

When I’m not writing or watching dramas, I love relaxing with soft music and a warm drink. Simple things like journaling at a café or organizing my idea notebook bring peace and spark new ideas.
